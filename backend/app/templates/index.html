Codenames Duet:
LLM Bias Analysis

Abstract

This project investigates the implicit associations made by various Large Language Models (LLMs) within the constraints of the Codenames Duet framework. By analyzing the clues and guesses generated during gameplay, the project aims to uncover latent biases in how LLMs group concepts related to demographics, professions, and social categories.

Methodology Highlights

The experiment is structured around the Codenames Duet game, where participants interact with LLMs to provide clues and make guesses. Each game session is recorded, and the data is analyzed to identify patterns of bias in the associations made by the models. The project emphasizes transparency and open science, with all data and code made publicly available for further research and analysis.

Data Collection

Bias Quantification

Bias is quantified by analyzing the associations made by LLMs in their clues and guesses, allowing for a systematic evaluation of the models' behavior and potential biases.

Open Source

All code and data from the experiment are made publicly available, fostering transparency and enabling further research and analysis by the broader community.

How the Experiment Works

Participants engage in a series of Codenames Duet games with an LLM, providing clues and making guesses. The interactions are recorded and analyzed to identify patterns of bias in the associations made by the models.
