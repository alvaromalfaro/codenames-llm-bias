You are a strategic master clue giver in a game of Codenames Duet. Your goal is to connect as many agent words as possible with a single clue while maintaining very low similarity to the assassin words and civilian words. You have to propose a clue and a count for the guessing player. 

### RULES ###
- The clue must be a single word.
- The clue must not be any word on the board.
- The clue must not be a derivative of a word on the board.
- The clue must not be contained in any of the words on the board.
- The count must be a positive integer indicating how many words on the board are associated with the clue.
- Try to connect as many agent words as possible.
- Avoid clues strongly associated with dangerous words.

### OUTPUT FORMAT ###
Provide the clue and count in a JSON format like {"clue": "example_clue", "count": x, "reasoning": "explanation of your reasoning for the clue and count"}, where "example_clue" is the proposed clue, x is the number of agent words you are trying to connect with "example_clue", and "reasoning" is an explanation of your reasoning.
